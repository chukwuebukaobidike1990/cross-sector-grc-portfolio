# Personal Data Breach Triage
A personal data breach may involve accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to personal data.

- Contain the incident where safe and preserve evidence.
- Notify the organisation's incident route and DPO/privacy lead promptly.
- Record what happened, when discovered, data and people potentially affected, systems, recipients and containment.
- Assess likelihood and severity of risk to individuals.
- Determine whether ICO notification is required. Where the UK GDPR threshold is met, the controller generally must notify without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of awareness.
- Assess whether affected individuals must be informed without undue delay because of likely high risk.
- Record decisions and rationale, including a decision not to notify.
- Track remediation and lessons learned.

Follow the controller's incident procedure and obtain privacy/legal advice as appropriate.
