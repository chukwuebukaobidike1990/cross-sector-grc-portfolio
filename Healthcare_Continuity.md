# Demonstration Case Study: Healthcare Continuity and Privacy
**Fictional scenario:** provider relies on hosted clinical system and specialist suppliers.

**Exercise outputs:** service dependency map; BIA prompts; identity, logging, backup and supplier controls; breach triage; management dashboard measures.

**Analysis:** clinical owners validate workarounds and recovery priorities. Security, privacy and clinical leadership coordinate incident decisions.

**Limitations:** no real provider assessed, no patient data used, no controls tested.
