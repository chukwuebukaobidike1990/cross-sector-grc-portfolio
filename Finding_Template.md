# Assurance Finding and Corrective Action
**Finding ID / title:**  
**Criteria:** policy, law, contract or control requirement.  
**Condition:** what the evidence demonstrated.  
**Cause:** validated root cause; distinguish fact from assumption.  
**Effect/risk:** actual or potential impact.  
**Evidence:** source, date, population/sample and limitations.  
**Rating:** method and rationale.  
**Management response:**  
**Corrective action:** specific and measurable.  
**Accountable owner / target date:**  
**Validation test:** how closure will be verified.  
**Closure decision:** independent reviewer and date.

Findings must be accurate, evidence-based, proportionate and traceable.
