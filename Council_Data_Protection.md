# Demonstration Case Study: Council Case-Management Platform
**Fictional scenario:** council uses outsourced platform containing resident information.

**Exercise outputs:** data-flow and dependency map; access/retention/incident risks; supplier assurance questions; breach triage; governance reporting.

**Lesson:** outsourcing does not remove the controller's accountability. Contract clauses, operational evidence and tested procedures must work together.

**Limitations:** fictional council and supplier; no legal compliance conclusion.
