# Charity and NGO GRC Playbook
**Risk landscape:** beneficiary safety, safeguarding, donor trust, restricted funds, volunteer access, cross-border operations and limited cyber resources.

**Priorities:** set proportionate governance; protect beneficiary/donor data; screen partners; maintain safeguarding escalation; protect restricted funds through approvals and reconciliations; prepare continuity for frontline services.

**Scenario:** volunteer emails beneficiary list to unintended recipient. Contain disclosure, notify privacy lead, assess safeguarding risks, document decisions and improve recipient checks.
