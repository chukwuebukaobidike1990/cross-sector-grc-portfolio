# Demonstration Case Study: Financial Services Third-Party Resilience
**Fictional scenario:** payments firm depends on external processor.

**Exercise outputs:** service impact analysis; supplier due diligence; incident escalation; continuity and reconciliation controls; exit planning; assurance-gap reporting.

**Lesson:** contractual SLAs alone do not demonstrate resilience. Test alternatives, data portability, reconciliation and exit arrangements.

**Limitations:** synthetic scenario; not a regulatory assessment or supplier review.
