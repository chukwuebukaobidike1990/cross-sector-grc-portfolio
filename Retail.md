# Retail and E-commerce GRC Playbook
**Risk landscape:** payment data, customer accounts, fraud, loyalty profiles, supply chain, peak trading availability, marketing consent and platform dependencies.

**Priorities:** determine PCI DSS scope; secure customer identity and privileged administration; govern consent and retention; monitor fraud; assess gateways and fulfilment partners; exercise peak-period continuity.

**Scenario:** credential-stuffing attack. Apply account protections, investigate exposure, assess personal data impact, coordinate communications and monitor recurrence.
