# Manufacturing and Critical Operations GRC Playbook
**Risk landscape:** operational technology, safety, production uptime, legacy systems, industrial suppliers, remote access and intellectual property.

**Priorities:** maintain IT/OT inventory and segmentation; govern vendor remote access; assess safety before OT changes; protect industrial backups; coordinate cyber response with engineering and safety; assess supply-chain concentration.

**Scenario:** supplier remote-access account suspected compromised. Restrict access safely, preserve logs, assess operational impact and verify secure re-enablement.
