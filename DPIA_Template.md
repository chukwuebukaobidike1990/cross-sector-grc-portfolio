# Data Protection Impact Assessment – Template
Use where processing is likely to result in high risk to individuals. Consult the DPO/privacy lead and confirm current UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 requirements.

## Processing description
Purpose; data subjects; data categories; special-category/criminal-offence data; sources; recipients; processors; transfers; retention; systems; scale and frequency.

## Necessity and proportionality
Purpose; lawful basis; additional condition where special-category data is involved; necessity of each field; less intrusive alternatives; transparency; rights; accuracy; minimisation and retention.

## Risks to people
Assess likelihood and severity of discrimination, identity theft, financial loss, physical harm, distress, confidentiality loss or loss of control.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Excess retention | Approved schedule and deletion checks | Data owner | TBD | TBD |

Record DPO advice, stakeholder consultation, unresolved high risks and whether prior ICO consultation is required. Approval does not remove controller accountability.
