# Education GRC Playbook
**Risk landscape:** safeguarding, children and vulnerable learners, student/staff data, identity fraud, online platforms, research data and examination availability.

**Priorities:** connect safeguarding, privacy and cyber governance; review staff/contractor access; assess edtech suppliers; define retention and sharing; test continuity for teaching and examinations; train staff on phishing and incident reporting.

**Scenario:** learning platform exposes a class list through an incorrect sharing setting. Contain access, establish scope, notify privacy leadership, assess risks to learners and verify remediation.
