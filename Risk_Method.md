# Enterprise Risk Method
Use a cause-event-consequence statement: “Because of [cause], [uncertain event] may occur, resulting in [impact on objectives].”

Likelihood and impact are each rated 1–5. Score = likelihood × impact.

| Score | Illustrative level | Response |
|---:|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low | Routine monitoring |
| 5–9 | Moderate | Owner action and periodic review |
| 10–16 | High | Formal treatment plan and management oversight |
| 17–25 | Critical | Prompt escalation and senior decision |

Assess inherent risk before controls. Assess residual risk only after considering controls that are implemented and supported by evidence. Record risk owner, treatment, milestones, dependencies, acceptance authority and review date. These bands are illustrative and must be calibrated to organisational appetite.
