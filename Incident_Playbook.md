# Cybersecurity Incident Response Playbook
**Prepare:** contacts, authority, logging, playbooks, exercises and evidence storage.  
**Identify:** validate alert, establish timeline, affected assets, scope and confidence.  
**Contain:** isolate or restrict systems proportionately; preserve evidence.  
**Eradicate:** remove root cause, persistence and compromised access.  
**Recover:** restore from trusted sources, validate integrity and monitor.  
**Learn:** analyse cause and impact, assess notification duties, track corrective actions.
