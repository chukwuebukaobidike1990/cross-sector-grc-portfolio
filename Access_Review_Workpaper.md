# User Access Review Workpaper
**Purpose:** Determine whether system access remains authorised and aligned to least privilege.

## Procedure
- Obtain a complete user and service-account population from the system owner.
- Reconcile to HR/identity records; identify leavers, movers, dormant and shared accounts.
- Confirm role and business need with accountable managers.
- Review privileged access separately, including emergency access.
- Record retain/modify/remove decision, approver and completion evidence.
- Verify changes were implemented; document exceptions and limitations.
