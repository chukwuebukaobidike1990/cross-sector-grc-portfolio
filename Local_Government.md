# Local Government GRC Playbook
**Risk landscape:** public service continuity, statutory duties, public funds, procurement integrity, safeguarding, privacy, cyber resilience and supplier dependency.

**Priorities:** align risks to corporate objectives; embed procurement conflict checks and due diligence; protect resident data; assess outsourced services; maintain delegated authority and audit trails; coordinate cyber, privacy, safeguarding and communications.

**Scenario:** outsourced case-management platform compromise. Establish service impact, protect individuals, invoke supplier/continuity clauses, preserve evidence, assess notification duties and report to governance.
