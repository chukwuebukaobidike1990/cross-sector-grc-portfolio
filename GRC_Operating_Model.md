# GRC Operating Model
## Purpose
Connect organisational objectives and obligations to accountable risk ownership, proportionate controls, evidence, assurance and continual improvement.

## Principles
- Business leaders own risks; GRC provides frameworks, advice, monitoring and challenge.
- Controls must be proportionate to risk, obligations and operational context.
- A policy is not proof that a control operates.
- Exceptions require documented rationale, owner, approval, compensating controls and expiry.
- Findings remain open until remediation is evidenced and validated.

## Three lines
**First line:** operational management owns processes, risks and day-to-day controls.  
**Second line:** risk, compliance, privacy and security functions set frameworks, advise, monitor and challenge.  
**Third line:** independent assurance provides objective assessment of governance, risk management and controls.

## Governance forums
| Forum | Responsibility | Suggested cadence | Outputs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Board | Strategy, appetite and oversight | Quarterly | Decisions and escalations |
| Risk Committee | Principal risks and compliance | Monthly/quarterly | Risk actions |
| Security & Privacy Group | Security posture, privacy and incidents | Monthly | Control decisions |
| Operational risk owners | Local risks and controls | Monthly | Updated register |
| Change Advisory Board | Material change decisions | As needed | Approvals and conditions |

## RACI
| Activity | Board | Business owner | GRC | IT/Security | Internal Audit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Set risk appetite | A | C | R | C | I |
| Own operational risk | I | A/R | C | C | I |
| Design security controls | I | C | C | A/R | I |
| Monitor compliance | I | R | A/R | C | I |
| Independent assurance | I | C | C | C | A/R |

R=Responsible, A=Accountable, C=Consulted, I=Informed. Adapt to actual delegations.

## GRC lifecycle
Understand context → identify obligations → assess inherent risk → select treatment → assign controls and owners → test design and operation → report residual risk → track remediation → review after change or incident.

## Policy lifecycle
Draft → stakeholder review → specialist review → delegated approval → publish and communicate → training/attestation → periodic review → retire and archive.
