# Third-Party Security and Compliance Assessment
## Supplier profile
Supplier; service; business owner; data processed; criticality; hosting locations; subprocessors; contract term; exit dependencies.

## Due diligence
- Corporate identity, ownership and financial resilience.
- Security governance and validity/scope of claimed certifications.
- Access control, MFA, encryption, vulnerability management, secure development and logging.
- Incident notification, cooperation and remediation commitments.
- Business continuity, disaster recovery, backup and restore tests.
- Privacy roles, processing instructions, subprocessors, transfers, retention and deletion.
- Personnel, physical security, insurance and regulatory obligations.
- Audit rights, change notification and exit/transition support.

## Illustrative scorecard
| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Data sensitivity | 20% |
| Service criticality | 20% |
| Security maturity | 25% |
| Resilience | 15% |
| Compliance assurance | 10% |
| Concentration and exit risk | 10% |

Define score anchors before use. Questionnaire answers are not independent assurance; validate material claims with evidence and contractual protections.
