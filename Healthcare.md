# Healthcare GRC Playbook
**Risk landscape:** patient safety, clinical availability, special-category health data, connected medical devices, supplier dependency, workforce access and ransomware.

**Priorities:** map clinical services and owners; assess privacy impacts; govern role-based and emergency access; assure EPR, hosting, diagnostics and device suppliers; test downtime procedures and recovery; coordinate cyber, clinical safety, privacy and communications response. For relevant NHS bodies/suppliers, assess DSPT and contractual requirements.

**Scenario:** clinical application outage during a busy shift. Prioritise patient safety, activate approved downtime procedures, establish incident command, preserve logs, communicate status and validate safe restoration. Technical recovery time is not automatically clinically acceptable.

**Evidence:** access reviews, DPIAs, device inventory, supplier assurance, restore tests, downtime exercises and lessons learned.
