# GRC Interview Practice
**GRC approach:** Start with objectives and obligations, identify material risks, agree owners, map proportionate controls and define evidence. Report residual exposure and track actions through verified closure.

**Inherent vs residual risk:** Inherent risk is assessed before controls. Residual risk is the exposure remaining after considering controls that are implemented and evidenced. A planned control is not an operating control.

**Control failure:** Validate facts and scope, assess impact, preserve evidence, contain, identify root cause, agree sustainable remediation, assign owner and due date, then retest.

**Supplier assurance:** Tier suppliers by criticality, data sensitivity and access. Validate claims, record risks, agree contract protections and refresh assurance after material change.

**Privacy and security:** Security protects confidentiality, integrity and availability. Privacy also assesses lawfulness, fairness, necessity, proportionality and individual rights.
