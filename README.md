Cross-Sector Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Portfolio
Portfolio owner: Chukwuebuka Obidike  
Focus: Governance | Enterprise Risk | Cybersecurity | Privacy | Third-Party Risk | Resilience | Assurance
Portfolio contents
`01_Governance/GRC_Operating_Model.md` — governance model, three lines, committees, RACI and policy lifecycle.
`02_Risk/Risk_Register.csv` and `Risk_Method.md` — sample enterprise risk register and scoring method.
`03_Cybersecurity/Control_Catalogue.csv` and `Access_Review_Workpaper.md` — control library and access review test.
`04_Privacy/DPIA_Template.md` and `Breach_Triage.md` — privacy assessment and breach response.
`05_Third_Party/Supplier_Assessment.md` — supplier due diligence and scoring.
`06_Resilience/BIA_Template.md` and `Incident_Playbook.md` — business continuity and cyber response.
`07_Assurance/Audit_Plan.csv` and `Finding_Template.md` — assurance planning and issue tracking.
`08_Metrics/GRC_Metrics.csv` — sample KPI/KRI catalogue.
`09_Sector_Playbooks/` — healthcare, financial services, local government, education, retail, manufacturing, charity and SaaS/cloud.
`10_Case_Studies/` — fictional cross-sector worked examples.
`11_Templates/` — policy, obligations register and control testing templates.
`12_Professional_Development/` — interview narratives and glossary.
Frameworks referenced
ISO/IEC 27001:2022; ISO/IEC 27002:2022; NIST CSF 2.0; NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5; ISO 31000:2018; ISO 22301:2019; UK GDPR; Data Protection Act 2018; PCI DSS v4.0.1; COSO Internal Control—Integrated Framework.
