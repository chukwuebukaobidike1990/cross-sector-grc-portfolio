# Financial Services GRC Playbook
**Risk landscape:** operational resilience, conduct, financial crime, fraud, confidentiality, model risk, outsourcing, third-party concentration and cyber threats.

**Priorities:** map important business services and applicable impact tolerances; maintain obligations inventory; assess outsourcing and exit arrangements; govern privileged access, payment controls and segregation of duties; define incident and regulatory escalation; validate board management information. Determine FCA/PRA applicability based on actual permissions and activities.

**Scenario:** payment processor outage. Assess customer impact, service tolerance, alternatives, reconciliation risk, supplier communications, notification triggers and remediation.
