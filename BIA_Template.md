# Business Impact Analysis Template
**Service/process:** [name] | **Owner:** [role] | **Review date:** [date]

Identify dependencies: people, premises, technology, data, suppliers and utilities.

Assess operational, financial, legal/regulatory, customer/patient safety, reputational and strategic impacts across 0–4h, 4–24h, 1–3 days, 3–7 days and beyond.

Record:
- Maximum tolerable period of disruption (MTPD)
- Recovery time objective (RTO)
- Recovery point objective (RPO)
- Minimum service level during disruption
- Manual workaround and maximum safe duration
- Upstream/downstream dependencies and single points of failure

Recovery targets require business approval and validation through exercises.
